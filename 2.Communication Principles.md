# Communication Principles — What I Learn't

Communication principles helped me understand what actually happens when one device sends data to another device across a network.

### Key points I learned

* Network communication requires a **sender, a receiver, and a medium** through which data travels.
* Data is broken into smaller units and transmitted across the network.
* Devices use **addresses** to identify where data should go.
* A switch uses **MAC addresses** to make forwarding decisions within a local network.
* The communication process can be observed using tools such as **Packet Tracer Simulation Mode**.
* Different types of traffic, such as **unicast, broadcast, and multicast**, are handled differently.

### What I observed in Packet Tracer

When I sent a packet between two PCs, I could follow its movement through the switch. I also observed the switch learning the PCs' MAC addresses and using its **MAC address table** when forwarding traffic.

### What I understood

I learned that network communication is not simply a device sending data directly to another device. **Different network devices examine information such as MAC addresses and use it to decide where the data should be forwarded.** This helped me understand what happens behind the scenes when devices communicate.
